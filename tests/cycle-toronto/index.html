Where gifts slip away

Getting a donor is hard. Losing one is quiet.

Nobody decides to let a donor go. It happens at small gaps along the way, like these.

  1. First gift
  2. The thank-you comes late, or not at all
  3. Second gift
  4. Nobody asks again
  5. Monthly giving
  6. A monthly gift lapses, and no one follows up
  7. Years of giving
  8. They lapse, and never hear from you

Fewer than 1 in 5 first-time donors give again. Every one who drifts away is a relationship you worked hard to start.

    I read how members and donors are thanked, renewed, asked and reported to.     The thank-yous and renewals are actually running, with a simple report your board can read. That's the start of the year, not the whole of it.
