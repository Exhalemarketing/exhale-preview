Why I do this

The business feels heavier than it should. That's usually money leaking.

Armand

I keep seeing the same pattern in companies that should, by every visible sign, be growing more smoothly than they are.

The offer is solid. The pipeline isn't empty. The founder is involved, sometimes too involved. And still, revenue slips away somewhere between the first good conversation and the point where a customer is properly settled.

It's rarely one dramatic failure. It's far more ordinary than that. People are busy, calls are happening, and the CRM looks fine as long as nobody looks too closely. That's why it's so hard to spot. It hides inside activity.

The first answer usually isn't more leads, more tools or another burst of marketing. Often it's a trusted, neutral, experienced pair of eyes to see where the drag is really coming from, and someone willing to stay and help fix it.
