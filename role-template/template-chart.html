Exhale role page template: the story arc, mapped to the hiring manager's questions

Built on the services page design. A hiring manager is not reading a resume. They want to know, in this order, whether the page is for them, whether she understood the posting, what she is actually offering, and whether she can do the work.

Custom  Filled from the posting, the resume, and the offer. Marked EXAMPLE in the template.
Fixed  Reused from the services page, word for word, so the two sites do not drift.
Optional  Personal connection (her yes, each time), personal video (top matches only), or the donor leak chart (donor roles only).
