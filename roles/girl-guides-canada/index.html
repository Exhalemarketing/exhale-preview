The first season

What I'd do in the first 90 days.

Here's the work I'd actually do in the first three months, alongside your people.

  1. Days 1 to 30

    Listen and map

    I spend time with the brand, the current campaigns, the team, the agencies and the request queue. I look at how work gets prioritized today. We agree what has to stay, what can wait, and which story the next stretch of work should serve.

  2. Days 31 to 60

    Connect the work

    A clearer way to prioritize requests, and separate initiatives pulled into one brand story. Social and digital direction follow that story. Someone on your side approves anything before it goes out.

  3. Days 61 to 90

    Make it a way of working

    The team has clearer ways of working, and the Chief Brand and Business Development Officer has a simple measurement view. Supporters start to meet the same Girl Guides in every channel.
