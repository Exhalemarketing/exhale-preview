Where funded volume slips away

Getting a sign-up is hard. Losing one is quiet.

Nobody decides to let a new client go. It happens in small gaps along the way, like these.

  1. Sign-up
  2. The sign-up waits too long for a next step
  3. Approval
  4. Approved, but the first invoice never goes in
  5. First funding
  6. One funding, then silence
  7. Repeat funding
  8. A good client drifts and nobody notices

Each of these gaps can be closed with a clear owner and a few well-timed messages. That's the work I'd want us to own.
