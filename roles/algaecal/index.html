Where customers slip away

Winning a customer is loud. Losing one is quiet.

Nobody decides to leave on day one. It happens at small gaps along the way, like these.

  1. First order or sign-up
  2. The welcome is generic, or late
  3. First real use
  4. They get stuck, and nobody notices
  5. The second decision
  6. The renewal or reorder moment passes in silence
  7. Years as a customer
  8. They drift away, and never hear from you

Each of those gaps is a moment a well-timed message can catch. That's the work.
