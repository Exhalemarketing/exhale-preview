What lands on a Communications Manager's desk at 4-H Canada.

  • Plans have to follow the programs, events, partners and the strategic plan, all at once.
  • The website, email, social, the Annual Report and sponsor recognition all go through one editor.
  • Provincial staff, volunteers, youth, donors, sponsors and alumni each need their own clear, timely message.
  • The brand and its licensing have to be protected while the work keeps moving.
  • The metrics are meant to improve next month's work, which only happens if someone looks at them.

A national organization supporting provincial partners needs a manager who'll do the writing, and the planning too.
