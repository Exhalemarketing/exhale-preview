Where support slips away

Getting a donor is hard. Losing one is quiet.

Nobody decides to let a donor go. It happens at small gaps along the way, like these.

  1. First gift
  2. The thank-you comes late, or not at all
  3. Second gift
  4. Nobody asks again
  5. Monthly giving
  6. A renewal stalls, and no one follows up
  7. Years of giving
  8. They lapse, and never hear from you

Fewer than 1 in 5 first-time donors give again. Converting a first gift into a second is still the hard part, and every one who drifts away is a relationship you worked hard to start.
