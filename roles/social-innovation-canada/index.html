From your posting

Your posting splits this role five ways. Here's how I'd spend the time.

Content creation and management, 40%

Newsletters, blog posts, reports and the website are the biggest slice of the job, so they'd get the biggest slice of my week. I write and edit myself. For design-heavy pieces I'd work from templates your team already uses, because Canva and Adobe aren't tools I list on my resume.

Communications strategy and delivery, 30%

I'd build an editorial calendar tied to your priorities, and give each project lead a simple way to ask for help. Their pieces can be bespoke and still sound like SI Canada.

Stakeholder and community engagement, 20%

I'd plan the network newsletter and targeted outreach as one rhythm, and bring partner feedback back into the next round. I haven't worked inside the social innovation field itself, so I'd lean on your team to learn who's who.

Media, and measurement, 5% each

Media work would be drafting and distributing releases and backgrounders, as the posting describes. Each month you'd get a short report on what people opened, read and answered, and what I'd change.
